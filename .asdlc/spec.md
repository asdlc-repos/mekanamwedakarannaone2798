# Overview

A leave management application where WSO2 employees request time off and managers approve or reject those requests.

# Personas

- Employee — requests leave and checks their balance
- Manager — approves or rejects leave requests from their team

# Features

- An employee submits a leave request by selecting dates and choosing a leave type.
- Managers see pending requests from people who report to them.
- A manager approves or rejects each request.
- Employees see the status of their submitted requests.
- Everyone can check how many leave days they have remaining for each type.
